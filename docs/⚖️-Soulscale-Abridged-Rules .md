## ⚖️ A tug-of-war for one human Soul

One side plays **Heaven** 😇, the other **Hell** 😈. Each round you both play a glyph into one of the Soul's five Chambers, and the Soul lurches up towards bliss or down towards despair. Quick, tactical, a little dice luck, and best with a friend in a voice channel.

How to play
- **Win:** drag the Soul to your end of the track: **+18** for Heaven, **−18** for Hell. Quick (±12) and Deep (±24) games too.
- **Pick:** each round you see 4 glyphs from your 10-glyph deck. Play one into a Chamber (🫀 Body, 🧠 Mind, ❤️ Heart, 🕰️ Time or 🪞 Memory) with an **Intent**.
- **Roll:** a d6 sets its strength. Spend 1 **Will** to reroll; you get 3 a round.
- **Clash:** pick the same Chamber as your opponent and the stronger play halves the weaker one's push.
- Both plays resolve together, then the Soul moves.

The four Intents
- **Press:** score now, boosted by your stack in that Chamber.
- **Anchor:** score nothing now, build a bigger stack.
- **Release:** cash in your stack for a burst.
- **Sever:** cut your opponent's stack before they cash it in.

Watch out for
- **Ongoing** glyphs that push every round, **Guards** that blunt the enemy, and **Traps** that ambush their next play.
- **Strain:** rolling 6s builds it. At 3, a **Purgatory Event** shakes the board.

Ways to play
- **vs Computer:** five personalities, from Novice to Cruel Accountant.
- **Duel:** challenge a friend in the same Activity, with secret picks. Anyone else can watch.
- **Pass & Play:** two players on one screen, with an optional secret mode.
- **Deck Builder:** make your own decks from 20 glyphs per side.
